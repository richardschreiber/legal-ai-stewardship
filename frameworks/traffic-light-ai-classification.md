# The Traffic Light AI Classification System
## Adopted Framework — ABA-Originated | AI Stewardship | Track 1: Legal & Organizational

---

## Definition

**This framework is not original Richard Schreiber IP.** The three-tier traffic-light model originates with the American Bar Association and has been adopted or referenced by multiple state bars and ethics committees. Richard Schreiber teaches and applies it; he did not author it.

**Primary source:** [ABA — Practical Checklist for Using AI Responsibly in Your Law Firm](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/law_practice/resources/law-technology-today/2026/checklist-for-using-ai-responsibly-in-your-law-firm/)

In Richard Schreiber's own published writing it appears as **"The Bar Classification System."** The name *Traffic Light AI Classification System* is the working label used across his content; it does not assert authorship.

The system is a three-tier classification of AI use cases for legal organizations. It gives attorneys and staff an immediate, intuitive framework for determining whether a specific AI use requires prohibition, human oversight, or standard approval. The tier assignments and the legal-organization deployment guidance below are applied and expanded by Richard Schreiber for the resource-constrained firm and legal services organization.

---

## The Three Tiers

### Red — Prohibited

These uses are prohibited without exception. No business justification, efficiency gain, or deadline pressure overrides the prohibition.

**Prohibited uses:**
- Inputting confidential client data into public AI tools without informed client consent and a reviewed data processing agreement
- Using AI for fact-finding — including legal research and citation generation — without independent human verification
- Representing AI-generated output as independently verified without conducting that verification
- Submitting AI-generated citations to a court or regulatory body without running the Six-Step Citation Verification Protocol

**Why these are absolute:**
Each prohibited use creates direct exposure — to the client, to the court, or to the regulator — that no efficiency gain justifies. The $110,000 sanction in Oregon was not the result of a bad lawyer. It was the result of a good lawyer who skipped the verification step under deadline pressure.

---

### Yellow — Requires Oversight

These uses are permitted with documented human review. The review is not optional — it is the governance requirement that makes the use permissible.

**Uses requiring oversight:**
- Legal research and citation finding (AI identifies; attorney verifies)
- Document review and initial drafting (AI drafts; attorney reviews and signs off)
- Any AI-generated content submitted to a court, regulatory body, or counterparty
- Client intake summaries generated by AI before attorney review
- AI-generated communications sent to clients under attorney name

**What oversight requires:**
- Named reviewer — not the attorney who ran the initial query
- Written verification log — what was verified, by whom, on what date
- Attorney sign-off before any Yellow-tier output leaves the organization

---

### Green — Standard Use

These uses are approved for standard deployment without additional review requirements beyond normal professional judgment.

**Standard uses:**
- Administrative tasks — scheduling, billing administration, internal communications
- Marketing content — social media, blog posts, website copy (reviewed before publication)
- Internal research summaries — not cited in legal work product
- Client-facing communications reviewed by a licensed attorney before sending

## How to Deploy This Framework

**For the managing partner:**
Post the Traffic Light system in every practice area. Train every attorney and staff member on the three tiers. The goal is not to restrict AI use — it is to make the governance boundary visible so every person in the firm knows which uses require oversight before they proceed.

**For the general counsel:**
Map every AI tool in your inventory to a tier. Red-tier tools that cannot be governed to Green or Yellow are removed from use. Yellow-tier tools get a named oversight owner before they go into production.

**For the legal aid managing attorney:**
The Traffic Light system is your one-page AI policy for a staff that has no time for a 40-page governance document. Print it. Post it. Review it at your next all-hands.

---

## What This Framework Replaces

The old approach: case-by-case judgment calls on AI use with no consistent organizational standard.

AI Stewardship approach: a clear, three-tier framework that makes the governance boundary visible to every person in the organization — before the output reaches a court, not after.
