# The Scarcity Scorecard
## Canonical Framework | AI Stewardship | Track 1: Legal & Organizational (Women-Led Firms)

---

## Definition

The Scarcity Scorecard is the diagnostic tool that maps the four leak zones where a managing partner's life and revenue are being silently drained: Time, Money, Physical/Mental Energy, and Emotional Bandwidth. Scoring: under 20 = green (mild seepage, common at growth plateaus); 20-40 = amber (leaks that will eventually undermine client service and personal sanity); above 40 = red (where burnout, divorce, or malpractice claims often sprout).

*"It is a diagnostic, not a diagnosis, an ally, not an audit."* — Law Partner in Heels, Chapter 2

---

## The Four Leak Zones

### Zone 1 — Time
The most democratic of currencies, escaping through orphan tasks only the managing partner — also the tech-support lead, HR director, and billing supervisor — ever sees.

### Zone 2 — Money
Vanishing in unbilled emails, slow-paying clients, and interest charges that masquerade as overhead.

### Zone 3 — Physical and Mental Energy
Siphoned by screen glare at midnight and adrenaline that never fully powers down.

### Zone 4 — Emotional Bandwidth
The invisible resource stretched thin whenever a tough negotiation bleeds into a family birthday, or a toddler's fever competes with a judge's deadline.

The zones overlap like water stains on a ceiling; left long enough, the plaster caves. But once named, the leaks can be traced — and anything traced can be repaired.

---

## The Instrument

Five quick pages. Ten plain-English questions. Zero judgment. No proprietary data required — only yesterday's lived reality: When did you start working? How many of those minutes were billable? Which task hit your energy hardest? Where, precisely, did guilt tap you on the shoulder? Completing it takes less time than scrolling the morning news; the insight rivals a full-day retreat.

The output is a color band, not a grade: green under 20, amber at 20-40, red above 40.

---

## What It Is Not

- It is not a practice-management audit. It measures the human running the firm, not the firm's software.
- It is not a one-time exercise. It is the baseline instrument the Time-Liberation Sprint and 90-Day CEO Ascend Blueprint measure progress against.
- It is not shame-based. The color bands exist to locate leaks, not to assign blame for them.
