# The Three-Gear Flywheel
## Canonical Framework | AI Stewardship | Track 1: Legal & Organizational (Women-Led Firms)

---

## Definition

The Three-Gear Flywheel is the "From Hours to Horizons" momentum framework from Law Partner in Heels, Chapter 3.3 — the engine that converts a diagnosed firm (see: The Scarcity Scorecard) into a moving one. Per the locked canonical definition, the three gears are: **pricing and equal-pay strategies, client-magnet funnels, and KPI dashboards and forecasting.**

In the book's narrative, Jen Morgan projects three great dials onto the frosted glass of her office before dawn — Time-Liberation, Leverage, Abundance — "each glowing like a compass rose... if those dials click together they will power a flywheel strong enough to move a firm, a family, and a life."

---

## The Three Gears

### Gear 1 — Pricing and Equal-Pay Strategies
The revenue gear. Repricing work to what it is worth — closing the gap between the value delivered and the fees captured, including the equal-pay gap women partners tolerate in their own pricing that they would never tolerate for a client.

### Gear 2 — Client-Magnet Funnels
The growth gear. Systematic client attraction replacing referral roulette — so the pipeline runs on architecture instead of exhaustion.

### Gear 3 — KPI Dashboards and Forecasting
The visibility gear. The numbers that matter — realization, pipeline, cash position — visible on demand instead of reconstructed at midnight. Forecasting converts the firm from reactive to directed.

A flywheel's physics apply: each gear is heavy to turn the first time, and nearly effortless once spinning. The gears compound — better pricing funds the funnel, the funnel feeds the dashboard, the dashboard proves the pricing.

---

## What It Is Not

- It is not a marketing funnel framework. Only one gear is about client acquisition.
- It is not sequential-only. The gears spin together; the framework's power is their interlock.
- It is not BigLaw practice-group strategy scaled down. It is built for the boutique firm where the managing partner is also the CFO.
