# The Five-Slide Board Briefing Deck
## Canonical Framework | AI Stewardship | Track 1: Legal & Organizational

---

## Definition

The Five-Slide Board Briefing Deck is the named deliverable for AI governance board readiness — ready to present on 24-hour notice. Five slides. No technical jargon. Everything a board needs to understand AI governance posture, ask the right questions, and ratify the right actions.

---

## The Five Slides

### Slide 1 — Regulatory Map
One line per jurisdiction on current compliance posture.

**What it shows:**
- Each active AI statute (Colorado, Texas TRAIGA, California, EU AI Act)
- Current compliance status: Compliant / In Progress / Gap Identified
- Enforcement timeline for each statute
- Cost of non-compliance per jurisdiction

**The board question it answers:**
*Where would we land in an enforcement action today?*

---

### Slide 2 — AI Inventory
Named systems. Named owners. Color-coded by risk tier.

**What it shows:**
- Every AI system in production — by name
- Named accountability owner for each system
- Risk tier: Red (high-risk) / Yellow (moderate) / Green (standard)
- Data processed by each system
- Vendor contractual status

**The board question it answers:**
*What AI systems are we running and who is responsible for each one?*

---

### Slide 3 — Policy Ratification Status
Department-by-department policy coverage.

**What it shows:**
- Which departments have an active, ratified AI use policy
- Date of last policy review per department
- Gaps: departments operating without ratified policy
- Recommended board action: ratify updated policy

**The board question it answers:**
*Is every department operating under a current, board-ratified AI use policy?*

---

### Slide 4 — Incident Response Readiness
Date of last rehearsed drill. Named scenarios covered.

**What it shows:**
- Date of last tabletop incident response exercise
- Scenarios rehearsed (hallucination reaching court, vendor breach, discriminatory output)
- Named roles in the incident response chain
- Date of next scheduled rehearsal

**The board question it answers:**
*If a vendor AI caused a breach tomorrow morning, are we ready?*

---

### Slide 5 — One Open Board Question This Quarter
The single most pressing governance question — with the cost of doing nothing.

**What it shows:**
- The governance decision that requires board input this quarter
- Two options: act now vs. wait
- Cost of each option — regulatory, reputational, operational
- Recommended board action

**The board question it answers:**
*What do we need to decide today?*

---

## How to Use This Framework

The Five-Slide Board Briefing Deck is not a presentation built from scratch for each board meeting. It is a standing deliverable — maintained quarterly, updated as governance posture changes, ready to pull off the shelf when a board question arrives or a regulatory deadline approaches.

The general counsel who can produce this deck on 24-hour notice is the general counsel who keeps the organization on the right side of the news cycle.

---

## What This Framework Replaces

The old approach: reactive board briefings assembled under pressure after an incident or enforcement action.

AI Stewardship approach: proactive standing documentation that demonstrates governance before a question is asked.
